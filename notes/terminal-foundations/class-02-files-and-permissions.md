# Class 2 - Files and Permissions

## What I Practised

- I used ls -l to view detailed information about folders and files.
- I learned that d at the beginning of a detailed listing means a folder, while - means a regular file.
- I read the permissions rwxr-xr-x for the practice folder.
- I read the permissions rw-r--r-- for notes.txt.
- I identified shadow as the owner who can change notes.txt.
- I used cat notes.txt to safely display the file's contents.

## What I Learned

Permissions control who can read, change, or use a file or folder. Limiting write access helps prevent accidental or unauthorised changes.
